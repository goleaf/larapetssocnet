PetSocial Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 23, 2026

PetSocial collects the information needed to create accounts, protect members, operate social features, and improve the reliability of the platform.

1. Information you provide

During registration, we collect your name, username, email address, password hash, date of birth, agreement confirmation, IP address, and user agent. After registration, you may add profile details, pet profiles, posts, photos, groups, messages, marketplace listings, event responses, and notification preferences.

2. Authentication and security data

We record security events such as registration, login attempts, verification email activity, password changes, session activity, and account restrictions. This data helps prevent account takeover, spam, automated abuse, and unauthorized access.

3. Date of birth

Date of birth is used for age eligibility and account safety. It is not shown publicly unless a later profile setting explicitly allows age-related display.

4. Cookies and sessions

PetSocial uses secure session cookies to keep you signed in, protect against cross-site request forgery, and support device session management. Session records may include user ID, IP address, user agent, payload, and last activity timestamp.

5. How information is used

We use account information to authenticate you, personalize your experience, enforce privacy settings, deliver notifications, process reports, support moderation, troubleshoot problems, and maintain platform safety.

6. Sharing

We do not sell account registration data. Information may be shared when you post it, when you choose visibility settings that allow others to see it, when required to operate service providers, or when necessary to comply with law and protect rights or safety.

7. Retention

We retain account and security records for as long as needed to operate PetSocial, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain audit integrity. Deleted accounts may remain in backups or security records for a limited period.

8. Your choices

You can update profile details, change privacy settings, manage notifications, log out other sessions, and request account deletion from settings. Some security records cannot be edited because they protect account integrity.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support channel listed in the application.
